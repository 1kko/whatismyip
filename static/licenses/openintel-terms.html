2. Attribution

If you make use of the open access data from the OpenINTEL project, you must acknowledge use of this data by including the following attribution in any document, web page or other communication:

  • "The research leading to these results was made possible by OpenINTEL (https://www.openintel.nl/), a joint project of the University of Twente, SIDN, NLnet Labs and SURF."

Furthermore, if you use data from the OpenINTEL project — for research that is published in a scientific paper, poster or other peer-reviewed content — you must cite one of the following papers.


In case your work uses Zonestream data, please cite:

  • Sommese, R., Akiwate, G., Affinito, A., Muller, Mm, Jonker, M., & Claffy, kc. (2024). DarkDNS: Revisiting the Value of Rapid Zone Update. In Proceedings of the 2024 ACM on Internet Measurement Conference (IMC '24). Association for Computing Machinery, New York, NY, USA, 454–461. https://doi.org/10.1145/3646547.3689021

In case your work uses RIR-level data, please cite:

  • Arouna, A., Livadariu, I., & Jonker, M. (2023). Lowering the Barriers to Working with Public RIR-Level Data. In Proceedings of the 2023 Applied Networking Research Workshop (ANRW '23). Association for Computing Machinery, New York, NY, USA, 24–26. https://doi.org/10.1145/3606464.3606473

In case your work uses Prefix Top List data, please cite:

  • Kastanakis, S., Fontein, R., Khadka, S.K., Jaw, E., Hesselman, C., Jonker, M. (2026). Prefix Top Lists Reloaded: A Temporal Prefix Ranking Dataset. In Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Passive and Active Measurement (PAM 2026). Online, 3-16. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-18268-5_1

In any other case please cite:

  • van Rijswijk-Deij, R., Jonker, M., Sperotto, A., & Pras, A. (2016). A High-Performance, Scalable Infrastructure for Large-Scale Active DNS Measurements. IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, 34(7), pp. 1877-1888. https://doi.org/10.1109/JSAC.2016.2558918

We like to keep track of research papers that use data from OpenINTEL on our website and would appreciate it if you can notify us of any publications in which you use our datasets. Please contact us and send us the citation of you paper, and, if possible a PDF of the paper so we can read it.

3. Use of the Download Service

Persons and/or organisations that wish to download data that is made available by the OpenINTEL project through its public servers must take reasonable measures to ensure that the project's servers do not experience undue load because of excessive downloads or automated processes. The OpenINTEL project reserves the right to block downloads from any location or address without prior notice. Any abuse will be reported to appropriate abuse contact points.


In general, we note that datasets are updated once every 24 hours, thus, downloading data more frequently than once per calendar day is generally not useful.
